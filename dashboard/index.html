⚙️  How UrjaSetu AI works — architecture & business logic

The 15-minute autonomy loop: every 15 minutes (simulated here in fast-forward), UrjaSetu (1) forecasts the next hours — occupancy from bookings/Wi-Fi, ambient temperature & humidity, rooftop solar, and the Time-of-Day tariff curve; (2) optimizes — solves for per-zone setpoints, battery charge/discharge and load-shifting that minimise cost subject to a hard comfort constraint (occupied zones must stay in the 23–26.5°C band, ECBC/ISHRAE-aligned); (3) acts — writes setpoints to AHUs/chillers and dispatches the battery inverter; and (4) verifies — compares expected vs actual power to detect faults (stuck valves, night wastage) within minutes, quantifying the ₹ lost and raising a work order.

Schneider-ready: the field layer speaks Modbus/BACnet — native citizens of Schneider Electric's EcoStruxure™ Building Operation. UrjaSetu is designed as an intelligence layer on top of EcoStruxure/Wiser deployments, not a competitor to them: Schneider sells the muscles, UrjaSetu is the brain that makes them 25–35% cheaper to run.

Demonstration mode: physics-based digital twin of a ~90,000 sq ft Mumbai commercial building (representative occupancy profiles, Mumbai climate, TOD-style tariff ₹6.2–₹13.4/kWh, grid factor 0.71 kg CO₂/kWh). In production UrjaSetu reads live meter & sensor data and writes controls via Modbus/BACnet/Wiser/EcoStruxure APIs. Built for the Yuva Yodha Energy Tech Hackathon 2026 by Team UrjaSetu.
